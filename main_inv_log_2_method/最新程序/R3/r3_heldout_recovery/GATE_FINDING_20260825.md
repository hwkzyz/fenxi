# R3 pre-formal gate finding

The initial 9+5 split held out 0.3 mm. Its static interpolation NRMSE was
2.82%, and the noiseless calibrated state-matched solver misidentified the
0.10 mm EO10 case as EO17 with 0.1685 mm amplitude. The formal Monte Carlo
must not use that split unchanged. The proposed pre-formal revision places
the distinct single-run 0.3 mm curve in the calibration family and holds out
1.4 mm instead; this keeps nine calibration and five bracketed operating
states while balancing old/new COMSOL sources.
